Residential solar

Solar for Brunei homes

Compare three options and find the one that suits your home.

Best value

Solar only

Panels + inverter, connected to the grid

Your home uses solar power first during the day. Any extra goes to the grid and is credited to your bill, kept for up to 12 months. At night you use the grid as normal.

Best for
The lowest cost and the quickest payback
Typical size
5–12 kWp (9–21 panels)
Indicative price
B$5,500 – B$13,000 installed
In a power cut
Switches off for safety, like any grid-tied system
Most independent

Solar + battery

Panels + hybrid inverter + home battery

Extra daytime solar charges a battery, which then powers your home in the evening when air-con use peaks. During a power cut, your lights, fridge and Wi-Fi keep running.

Best for
Evening-heavy homes, home businesses, areas with power cuts
Typical size
5–12 kWp + 5–15 kWh battery
Indicative price
Solar price + about B$4,000 – B$6,000 per 10 kWh of battery
In a power cut
Backup circuits keep running on solar and battery
Flexible

Battery-ready

Start with solar, add a battery later

We fit a hybrid inverter now, so adding a battery later is a simple plug-in upgrade with no rewiring. Ideal if you want to spread the cost or wait for battery prices to fall further.

Best for
Keeping today's cost low while staying future-proof
Typical size
5–12 kWp, battery added any time
Indicative price
Solar price + a small extra for the hybrid inverter
In a power cut
Backup becomes available once a battery is added
