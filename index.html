Questions we hear a lot

Is solar worth it when electricity in Brunei is subsidised?

It depends on how much you use and when. Homes with high usage and businesses with daytime loads see the best returns. Many customers also value backup power, lower emissions and protection against future tariff changes. Our estimate above is deliberately conservative, and we'll tell you honestly if solar doesn't make sense for your site.

Do I need approval to install solar?

Grid-connected systems need approval from the Department of Electrical Services. We prepare and submit the application and technical drawings for you.

What happens during rain and at night?

Panels still produce some power on cloudy days. At night you draw from the grid as usual, or from a battery if you add one.

Which equipment do you use?

Tier-1 panels and established inverter brands, each with manufacturer certifications (IEC 61215 / 61730) and warranties. Your quote lists the exact models.

Can I pay monthly?

We are working with local banks on Shariah-compliant financing, and we offer pay-per-kWh plans for businesses. Ask us about the current options.

How much maintenance is needed?

Very little: periodic panel cleaning and an annual electrical check. Monitoring alerts us if output drops unexpectedly.
