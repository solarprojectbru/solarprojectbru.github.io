Why Brunei Solar

Local expertise & honest outcomes

A clear look at how we take the guesswork, paperwork and risk out of going solar in Brunei.

Advice

Honest numbers

Conservative savings estimates using Brunei's real tariff bands. If solar doesn't suit your home, we'll say so.

Paperwork

Approvals handled

We prepare your net-metering application, engineering drawings and approvals so you don't have to.

Equipment

Quality you can rely on

Tier-1 panels and established inverter brands with IEC certifications and manufacturer warranties.

Monitoring

Live on your phone

Watch your output and savings in an app. We get an alert if anything needs attention.

Local team

Bruneian-led, all four districts

We understand local homes, roofs, rules and culture, from Brunei-Muara to Temburong.

Aftercare

With you for decades

Cleaning and check-up plans keep your system performing for its 25-year life.
