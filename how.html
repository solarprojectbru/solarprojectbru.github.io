How it works

From first visit to switch-on

Five simple steps, usually 6–10 weeks depending on approvals.

  1. 1

    Free site survey

    We check your roof, shading, wiring and 12 months of electricity bills.

  2. 2

    Design & quote

    A system sized to your usage, with a clear price, expected output and payback.

  3. 3

    Approvals

    We prepare the net-metering application and engineering drawings for the authorities.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Usually 1–3 days for a home. Tidy cabling, safe roof work, full testing.

  5. 5

    Monitor & care

    Watch your output live on your phone. We check and clean the system on a schedule.
